Why AI safety

Why this matters

AI systems are becoming more capable at a remarkable pace. Making sure they remain safe, understandable, and beneficial is one of the most important challenges of our time.

Capabilities are outpacing understanding

Today's AI models can write code, reason about complex problems, and act autonomously, yet we still understand surprisingly little about how they work internally or how to reliably control them.

The stakes are high

As AI is deployed in critical areas like science, infrastructure, and decision-making, failures or misaligned objectives could cause serious harm. Getting this right early matters.

It's a tractable, growing field

Alignment, interpretability, evaluations, and governance are active research areas where talented people can make real contributions, and the field needs more of them.

Munich can contribute

With strong universities and a growing tech scene, Munich has the talent to matter in AI safety. A local community makes it easier to get started and stay engaged.
