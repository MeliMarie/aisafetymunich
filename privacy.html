Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit safeaimunich.org or register for the Munich AI Safety Student Initiative, why we do it, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Registration form (Student Initiative)

What we collect

  • Name and university email address
  • Phone number, only if you choose to give it
  • Degree and university
  • The branches you are interested in
  • Your answer to why you are interested in the club
  • Your consent and the time you submitted the form

Why

To add you to the branches and reading groups you picked, invite you to sessions and events, and contact you about the Student Initiative. If you give a phone number, we only use it to add you to the club's group chat. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the box on the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.

Who receives it

Only the club leads and organisers who coordinate the branches. To deliver the form to our inbox we use Web3Forms, which acts as our data processor. Web3Forms runs on Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare, so your data may be processed outside the EU, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

As long as you are an active member, and at most until the end of the academic year after your last contact with us. We review the list at the start of each semester and delete entries of people who have left. If you ask us to delete your data, we do so within 30 days.

3. Visiting the website

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., USA. When you open a page, GitHub's servers process technical data such as your IP address, the page requested, date and time, and browser information, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts and scripts loaded from third parties

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) and JavaScript libraries from the unpkg content delivery network. To do this, your browser connects to their servers, which receive your IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest in presenting the site consistently.

Embedded calendar

Once enabled, the Student Initiative page shows an events calendar from Luma (Luma AI Inc., USA). Loading it sends your IP address and browser information to Luma. See the Luma privacy policy.

Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email

Links to our WhatsApp group or other platforms only send data to those services when you click them. From then on, their own privacy policies apply.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies and do not use analytics or tracking tools.

4. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the correspondence once it is no longer needed.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18)
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • withdraw your consent at any time (Art. 7(3))

Email hello@safeaimunich.org to use any of them. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. For private bodies in Bavaria this is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, www.lda.bayern.de.

6. Changes

We update this policy when the website or our tools change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
