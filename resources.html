Resources

A curated starting point for getting into AI safety, from first introductions to courses and career guidance.

Start here

Accessible introductions if you're new to the field.

  • AI safety support

    A community-building project working to reduce the likelihood of existential risk from artificial intelligence by providing resources, networking opportunities and support to early career, independent and transitioning researchers.

  • The Safety Apprentice

    A guide for people new to the field, covering everything we wish someone had told us when we started.

  • AISafety.info

    A large, beginner-friendly FAQ answering common questions about AI safety and alignment.

  • AISafety.com

    A map of the AI safety ecosystem: communities, orgs, funders, courses, and events.

  • 80,000 Hours: Risks from AI

    An in-depth problem profile explaining why advanced AI might pose serious risks and what can be done.

Courses & programs

Structured ways to build up knowledge and skills.

  • AI safety trainings

    An on overview of technical, governance and general upskilling programs.

  • SAIGE

    A fellowship at Germanys first national AI safety org - coming soon!

Research & reading

Where current research and discussion happen.

  • Alignment Forum

    The main online hub for technical AI alignment research and discussion.

  • AI Safety Report

    A review of the latest science on capabilities and risks of general-purpose AI systems written by over 100 independent experts.

  • UN Report of the Independent International Scientific Panel on AI

    A preliminary evidence-based assessment of AI's opportunities, risks and impacts from the UN's newly convened scientific panel, written for a global policy audience.
